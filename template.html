Gear

Click any slot to change it; right-click to take the item off. Changes show against the Adept's current gear (green better, red worse). Taking a one-of-a-kind item from another Adept gives them your old one if they can use it. The totals compare against your baseline.

Djinn

Click a djinni to put it on Standby, swap it or remove it (right-click removes it at once); click + to add one (up to 9 each). Each option shows the class it leads to and the change in stats. Taking a djinni from another Adept gives them the one you swapped out. Red name = Standby (ready to summon: no class or stat effect), dotted = not collected in your imported save.

How the numbers are worked out
  • Class is picked from each Adept's elemental levels: 5 in their own element plus one per Set djinni. The element with the highest level leads, ties go to the Adept's own element, and the highest class tier whose requirements are met wins. This reproduces every class table on the Golden Sun Universe wiki, including partial classes like Ivan as a Ninja.
  • A few odd mixes (for example a Venus Adept with one Jupiter and one Mars djinni, or djinn of all four elements without a class item) aren't covered by the wiki. Those show a "best guess" warning.
  • Base stats follow the game's growth curve: six points per stat, read as levels 0, 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100 with straight lines between them. Checked against 248 Adepts in real saves from both games, this leaves no bias; level-ups add a small random amount, so real stats sit a few points either side.
  • The Broken Seal uses the same class, stat and item tables as The Lost Age, limited to its four Adepts, 28 djinn (7 per Adept at most) and its own items. Sol Blade, Masamune and Kusanagi are left out there: they exist only as unused data in the first game. The Medium line is called Medium, Dark Mage, Death Mage in the first game. The first game has no class items.
  • Final stat = (base + Set djinn bonuses + stat items + flat gear bonuses + your modifiers) × class percentage, then × any gear multiplier (Elven Shirt, Adept's Helm and the like). The wiki confirms weapon and armor Attack/Defense are added before the class percentage; djinn and other flat bonuses are assumed to work the same way.
  • Stat items use the amount stored in the game's item table: Power Bread +8 HP, Cookie +6 PP, Apple +5 Attack, Hard Nut +3 Defense, Mint +5 Agility, Lucky Pepper +2 Luck. If the game rolls a random amount, these may be the most you can get rather than a fixed gain.
  • Elemental Power = 75 + the Adept's affinity for that element (4 own, 3 neutral, 2 opposed, 1 symbiotic, from the character table) + 5 per elemental level + gear, capped at 200. That gives 104 in the Adept's own element with no djinn, matching the wiki. Resistance works the same way from a base 10 higher. Without gear, both formulas match the values stored in real saves from both games (every Adept checked). The game stores these without equipment bonuses, so gear Power/Resistance bonuses are added on top here, as the item descriptions say, but that part can't be checked against a save.
  • Battle damage follows the game's own code (The Broken Seal's battle routines in the Alchemy decompilation, with the tables read from the ROMs). Elemental difference = the attacker's Power minus the target's Resistance in the attack's element, capped at ±200. Attack, unleashes and "Attack +n" Psynergy: (Attack − Defense + 2 × power) ÷ 2 × (1 + difference ÷ 400). Psynergy: power × (1 + difference ÷ 200), then 100/80/60/40/20% (or 100/70/40/30/20%, or 100/50/30/10%) for each step away from the target aimed at. Every hit adds 0 to 3 at random. A plain Attack takes the element of the equipped weapon (12 weapons have one), none without it. The Lost Age is assumed to work the same way: it has the same curve and spread tables, but its battle code isn't decompiled yet. Criticals, misses and buffs aren't included.
  • Unleash chance comes from the weapon (35%, or 40% for a few) plus gear such as Hyper Boots. The game describes those as boosting "Criticals"; the wiki lists them as unleash-rate bonuses.
  • Class items (Mysterious Card, Trainer's Whip, Tomegathericon) get their own slot here. I couldn't confirm whether the game makes them share a slot with rings.
  • "Endgame gear only" uses a hand-picked list per slot, chosen from each item's full stats and effects rather than Defense or Attack alone (so Hyper Boots and Ninja Sandals make it for their unleash bonus, and rings are judged by what they do in battle). An item you already have equipped stays in the list, marked "not endgame".
  • Equipping an item another Adept is wearing takes it from them when only one copy exists, and gives them your old item if they can equip it. Items sold in shops are treated as unlimited; with a save imported, your actual number of copies is used.
  • Dark Dawn: class lines, djinn requirements, class percentages, Psynergy by level and djinn bonuses come from the Golden Sun Syndicate's Dark Dawn class and djinn guides, read per Adept. How the game picks between lines isn't published; the planner uses the same rule as the GBA games, which agrees with the GBA class choice in 98% of djinn mixes. Base stats are entered by hand (growth curves aren't published). Psynergy figures (PP, power, range) are borrowed from The Lost Age where the names match; 21 Dark Dawn-only Psynergy show no figures. Elemental Power/Resistance isn't modelled yet, so gear Power/Resist bonuses are listed but not added to anything.
  • Monsters (The Broken Seal and The Lost Age): read straight from the game ROMs (stats, elemental tables, moves, EXP, coins, drops; names from the game text). The Lost Age figures match the Archipelago randomizer's export field for field and the Golden Sun wiki on every enemy checked. Drop chance is 1/2 to the power (code − 1), checked against the wiki's 1/256 for the Cruel Dragon's Tisiphone Edge; defeating an enemy with a Djinn attack can raise it. The Lost Age's table also holds copies of Broken Seal enemies; ones identical to a Broken Seal record are marked as likely unused there, since the encounter tables aren't read yet.
  • Dark Dawn gear: the 188 weapons and armor (stats, effects, unleash names, prices, locations) come from Golden Sun Realm's Dark Dawn weapon and armor guides; not checked against the game. Unleash rates and power aren't published, and effects given only as words ("More critical hits", "Boosts Earth Power") aren't counted. Who can equip what follows the Golden Sun wiki's character pages by item type; items marked "may not be equippable" are where the wiki says a type is limited without naming items (Karis's staffs, Sveta's robes and headgear, Amiti's crowns) and goggles, which no page lists. Boots and rings are assumed to fit everyone.
  • Field-only Psynergy that comes from items (Move, Lift, Teleport and so on) isn't listed.
